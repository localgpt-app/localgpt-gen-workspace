# River Crossing Region

The western boundary of the village, defined by the river that flows north-to-south. The stone bridge connects the village center to the forest edge path. The blacksmith forge sits on the near bank, its orange glow and hammer-ring audible across the water.

## Layout

```
     Forest Edge ↑
         │
    ─────┤ bridge ├─────  (river flows S→N)
         │        │
  Cottage 4      Forge
  (near bank)    + Kira NPC
         │
  Cottage 5      Pond
  (waterwheel)   (quiet water)
         │
     ─── river continues south ───
```

## Water Bodies

### River
- Generated by `gen_add_water` in Phase 1
- Height: Y = 0.5
- Size: [80, 15] — runs full length of world
- Color: "#2a6e4f" (dark forest green)
- Opacity: 0.65
- Wave speed: 0.5
- Audio: water emitter (turbulence 0.4, radius 12, volume 0.5)

### Pond
- Position: [-10, 0.3, 2]
- Shape: Flat cylinder (radius 3, height 0.05)
- Color: [0.15, 0.35, 0.55, 0.7], alpha_mode "blend"
- Metallic: 0.3, Roughness: 0.1
- Audio: water emitter (turbulence 0.2, radius 6, volume 0.25)
- 3 lily pads: flat green planes (scale [0.3, 0.01, 0.3]) floating on surface, gentle bob (amplitude 0.01, frequency 0.15)

## Structures

### Stone Bridge
- Position: [-5, 0.6, 10]
- Deck: Cuboid (6, 0.3, 3), stone gray [0.5, 0.48, 0.45, 1], roughness 0.8
- Left wall: Cuboid (6, 0.6, 0.2), same stone, position offset z -1.3
- Right wall: Cuboid (6, 0.6, 0.2), same stone, position offset z +1.3
- Collider: box on deck + walls (static)
- **Design note:** Bridge must be wide enough for the player avatar (collision_radius 0.3) to cross comfortably. The 3-unit width provides ample clearance.

### Blacksmith Forge
- Position: [-5, 0, 5]
- Body: Cuboid (4, 3, 3.5), color [0.3, 0.2, 0.12, 1] (dark timber, smoke-stained)
- Roof: Pyramid (base 4.5×4, height 1.5), very dark [0.1, 0.08, 0.06, 1]
- Open front: no wall on south face — the forge interior is visible
- Anvil: small cuboid (0.4, 0.4, 0.3), dark metallic [0.2, 0.2, 0.2, 1], metallic 0.9, roughness 0.3
- Forge fire pit: small cuboid (0.6, 0.3, 0.6), dark with emissive [0.8, 0.3, 0.05, 0.6]
- Chimney: cylinder on roof (dark stone), with 2 tiny gray spheres bobbing upward (smoke puffs, path_follow up, speed 0.3, mode "loop")
- Point light inside: warm orange [1, 0.6, 0.2, 1], intensity 600, range 5
- Fire audio emitter: intensity 0.8, crackle 0.6, radius 5, volume 0.4
- Door: `gen_add_door(entity "forge_door", trigger "proximity", open_angle 110, auto_close false)`
- Collider: box on body (static)

### Forge Master Kira (NPC)
- Position: [-5, 0, 5.5] (standing at anvil)
- Behavior: "idle"
- Model: "default_humanoid"
- Dialogue: trigger "click", radius 2.5
  - Node "greeting": "The forge runs hot today. If you need a blade sharpened, come back tomorrow. I'm working on something... special."
  - Node "hint": "I saw something glinting under the old bridge last week. Gold, maybe? Couldn't reach it myself."

### Cottage 4 — "The Fisherman's Shanty"
- Position: [-12, 0, 6]
- Body: Cuboid (2.5, 2, 3), color [0.5, 0.35, 0.2, 1]
- Roof: Pyramid, dark brown
- Fishing rod: thin cylinder leaning against wall (angled, brown)
- Barrel: cylinder (radius 0.3, height 0.5, brown) next to door
- Door: `gen_add_door(trigger "click")`
- Collider: box (static)

### Cottage 5 — "The Miller's House" (with waterwheel)
- Position: [-14, 0, 0]
- Body: Cuboid (3, 2.5, 3), color [0.55, 0.4, 0.25, 1]
- Roof: Pyramid, dark
- Waterwheel:
  - Wheel: Torus (major_radius 1.2, minor_radius 0.15), dark brown [0.3, 0.18, 0.08, 1], position [-14, 0.8, -1.5]
  - Axle: thin cylinder through torus center, attached to cottage wall
  - Behavior: spin (axis [1, 0, 0], speed 15 deg/s) — slow, steady rotation
  - **Design note:** The waterwheel is a signature visual element. It should be visible from the bridge approach.
- Collider: box on body (static)

### Medallion #3
- Position: [-5, 0.3, 10.5] (tucked under the bridge deck — player must walk to the riverbank and look underneath)
- Shape: Torus (scale [0.15, 0.15, 0.15])
- Emissive: [0.9, 0.7, 0.1, 1]
- Behaviors: spin + bob
- Collectible: value 1, category "medallions"
- **Hint source:** Forge Master Kira's dialogue mentions "something glinting under the old bridge"

## Audio Zones

The river crossing region has the densest audio layering in the world:
- River water emitter (always audible from this region, fades toward village center)
- Pond water emitter (quieter, local)
- Forge fire emitter (directional from forge interior)
- Waterwheel: custom emitter (waveform "brown_noise", filter_cutoff 300, filter_type "lowpass", radius 6, volume 0.1) — creaking wood sound
